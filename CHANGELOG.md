# Changelog

## 2026-09-30
- Organized the repository into folders (`assets`, `css`, `data`, `js`, `tools`, `docs`), with a README, a development guide and a content guide.

## 2026-09-29
- Home: a big Start button names the next part of today's plan. On the plan cards, the current part has a filled Start pill and later parts are dimmed.
- Appearance: dark (the default), light, or match the device.
- Parent Feedback report: skills that need support now (with evidence and at-home ideas), what is going well, and observations on attention, feelings and routine.
- Adaptive practice: up to 3 focus skills shape each day's review questions, and every 4th day one part reviews a learned focus topic. The day's size never changes.
- Question bank versions that never break saved progress, plus a release check (`tools/bank-check.js`).
- Question bank v2: 698 new items (432 language, 112 People Lab, 50 Brain Skills, 102 vocabulary words for Grades 3–5), each checked by a second reviewer.
- Expert words: one real above-grade word with every Discovery card, a one-question Expert check, stamps, and real job titles.

## 2026-09-27
- One small step a day: the daily plan is locked, and a Discovery card is earned for each finished day, with gold cards and tomorrow's preview.
- 17 interests; a curriculum filter for parents.
- One green for right answers and one red for wrong answers everywhere.
- Phase 2: mastery, spaced review, frustration signals, Brain Skills (66 items), weekly report; Grades 4–5 language bank.
- Phase 1: several children, K–5 math generators, K–3 language bank, People Lab by grade, voice mode for Kindergarten, parent dashboard. Named Learning Quest.
- Daily plan with words, math and people; profile and math curriculum based on Singapore Math and RSM.

## 2026-09-26
- First version: a grammar practice game, then Number Lab, People Lab, a daily visual schedule and Calm Corner.
